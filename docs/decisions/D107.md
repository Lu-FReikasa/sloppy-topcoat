# D107 — Topcoat has its own wiki; Outline is removed

**Decision.** Topcoat keeps its documentation in its **own wiki**, built on the Ablage (D102): a page is a report of the
application `wiki` (immutable versions, diff, `released_version`) plus a row in `wiki_pages` that gives it a section, a
title and the path `section/title` (`hub/src/topcoat_hub/wiki_store.py`). The external **Outline** wiki stays where it is,
but Topcoat no longer reads, indexes or comments on it; its knowledge sources and the notes mirrored from them were deleted
(migration `wk01_outline_out`). Wrong data does more harm than none.

**Format.** Markdown is the source, extended by rendered blocks (first ` ```mermaid `, later charts and images). No HTML as
a writing format: models write Markdown more reliably, every check works on text, diffs stay readable.

**How a page changes.** Only through a project and a person's click (D15): "take into the wiki" on a document of a task
(`report_kind == "document"`) first shows a card (target page, new page or next version, length); the click on it makes
the new page or version, and that version *is* the page from then on. There is no second release step (the maintainer,
2026-11-12, after looking at the first two pages: a separate "release" added nothing) and no hand editor. Earlier versions stay readable
with their diff.

**Who reads.** At first only the assistants of Projekte and Verwaltung, through the read-only tools `wiki.list` and
`wiki.read`, every page in its current version. Chat, research and development have no access yet. Wiki pages are not put into
the knowledge base (they would reach every application through `knowledge.*`), and neither are the documents of doc
programs (`doc_section`), which are drafts of wiki pages.

**Decided with the maintainer on 2026-11-12** (plan `2026-11-12-plan-wiki-anwendung.md` in the docs folder).
