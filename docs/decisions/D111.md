# D111 — The inbox has no marks, no board and no re-check

> **Status (2026-11-25):** Decided by the maintainer on 2026-11-24 (plan of 2026-11-24, E1); built 2026-11-25 (plan step 1.16, migration `ib01_inbox_without_marks`). Replaces [D68](D68.md) and the board and pinning of [D61](D61.md).

**Decision.** The marks on inbox items (Post-it, highlight, sticker, comment, author `recheck`), the filing of read items ("Ablegen" / "Abgelegt"), the board of pinned items, pinning itself and the regular re-check of pinned answers are removed: code, API and the stored marks. The inbox keeps what happened and wants to be read, each item with its application, as [D61](D61.md) describes otherwise. A database dump comes before the migration.

**Why.** The maintainer did not use them and asked for them to go "completely". A pinned answer that a background job re-checks costs model time for a claim nobody asked about again, and a second place to keep items (the board next to the inbox) splits attention without adding information.
