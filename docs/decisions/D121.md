# D121 — A check comes from another model family where there is one

> **Status (2026-11-25):** Built as step 1.14 of the plan of 2026-11-24 (`chat_review.pick_reviewer`, `model_profiles.same_family`). Amends [D87](D87.md). Not yet seen live.

**Decision.** The model that checks a result counts the author's whole model family as the author's own model: a quantisation or a size of the same family shares its blind spots. The checker is chosen in this order, and a check never pushes a model out or loads on a busy node:

1. a loaded model of another family, on a node other than the author's;
2. a loaded model of another family, on the author's node;
3. the author's model, loaded, on another node;
4. a model of the admin's check order that is not loaded, on a node that holds no loaded model and is not the author's;
5. the author's model on the author's node.

The record of the check says whether it was the author's model. [D87](D87.md) still holds in its core: a check needs a fresh context; a second model is better where there is one.

**Why.** Seen in issue 2.19: the reviewer fell back to the author's own model (gpt-oss checked gpt-oss), and a model that checks its own family's work shares its blind spots. The last place keeps [D88](D88.md): a machine with one model still runs every chain, with a weaker check.
