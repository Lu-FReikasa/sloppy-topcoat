# D127 — Voice: speech in and out through node services, half duplex first

> **Status (2026-11-25):** Decided by the maintainer (plan of 2026-11-24, C5 and recommendation 16; taken into the roadmap now, before the secretary). Not built: milestone *P7 Voice*.

**Decision.**

- **Services.** Speech to text with `whisper.cpp` and text to speech with Piper run as services of a node (a CPU node such as a laptop is enough), pinned official releases verified by hash, behind interfaces with fakes for tests. Not on the hub's own container ([D7](D07.md)).
- **Audio path.** The browser or the desktop client records while the person holds a key (push to talk) and plays the answer; audio travels over the person's TLS connection to the hub and through the node session to the service. Half duplex first: listen, then speak.
- **A channel, not a program.** Voice is a way to talk to the assistant: first the chat of today, later the secretary ([D112](D112.md)), with "connect me with Administration" and back. Each persona can have its own voice.
- **No confirmation by voice.** A spoken "yes" never confirms an action; the voice names the card, the person confirms by click or key ([D15](D15.md), [D83](D83.md)).
- **Privacy.** Audio is not stored; the transcript is a normal message of the conversation; in incognito nothing is written.

**Why.** The maintainer wants to talk to Topcoat. Building the voice path now against today's chat means it works before the secretary exists and needs no change when the secretary takes over the conversation.
