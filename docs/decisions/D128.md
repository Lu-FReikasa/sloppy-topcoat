# D128 — Development makes personas and themes from a prompt; the person installs them

> **Status (2026-11-25):** Decided by the maintainer. Not built: milestone *Personas and themes by Development*.

**Decision.** The Development application can turn a short prompt into a **persona** or a **theme**:

- **Persona:** text from the persona building blocks ([D38](D38.md)), a figure (pixel look first, [D126](D126.md)) with its moods, lines per mood in German and English, and later a voice ([D127](D127.md)).
- **Theme:** a Theme v2 document ([D113](D113.md)) with CSS and assets ([D114](D114.md)), after a short research of inspirations (ideas, frameworks, GUIs).

Both are made as **drafts of data plug-ins** ([D105](D105.md)) in the workshop, which is scratch: making, changing and previewing a draft needs no click. Development shows the person screenshots of the draft (wide, narrow, overlay, the moods of a figure) and takes the person's feedback for the next iteration. **Installing or activating** a draft is the person's click in the registry, with version and rollback. A persona changes the tone of answers, never their content: evidence, warnings and approval cards stay factual.

**Why.** The maintainer wants many creative personas and themes and wants Development to work freely inside a safe frame and hand over screenshots to judge. Drafts as plug-in data keep everything checkable and reversible; the click on install keeps the person in charge of what the interface becomes.
