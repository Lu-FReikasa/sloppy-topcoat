# D54 — A section is a chat of its own

**Decision.** **A section is a chat of its own (user area).** Supersedes the *cut inside one chat* of D53 for new conversation. The continuous conversation is a chain of ordinary chats: `conversations.continues_id` (migration 0050) points at the chat a chat continues. Before every message the page asks `POST /api/conversations/{id}/section` (`force` = the person's "new section"): the same chat while it was used within `segment_idle_seconds`, otherwise a new chat that continues it and keeps model, node, quality, server choice and persona. The prompt of the new chat holds only its own messages plus the hand-over (summary of what the nearest chat ended with, failing that its digest, failing that its verbatim end) and recalls from the summaries of the whole chain (`SegmentService._chain_plan`); the chat just left is summarised in the background. `GET /api/conversations/{id}/thread` lists the chain (newest 8 before the open chat) with the summaries, which the page shows as one history with a line and the summary between chats; the archive lists every link as a normal chat marked "continuation". Chats from before stay as they are and keep the in-chat cut (`segment_breaks`, D53). Nothing is moved or deleted.

**Why.** One system for chats and sections: archive, search, export and delete need no special case, and topic chats (later) are the same mechanism. The chain is created lazily when a message is written, so no empty chats appear, and the in-chat cut remains as the fallback for old long chats.
