# D61 — One interface, applications and the inbox

> **Status (2026-11-25):** Amended. The board and pinning go with [D111](D111.md).

**Decision.** **One interface, applications and the inbox (P1 of docs/PLATFORM.md).** The classic pages, the dock and the admin side bar are gone: the interface is the workspaces (D60), and an address of the old interface is moved into a workspace which opens the window that owns the page. The built-in workspaces are the **applications** Chat, Research, Development and Administration (Homelab is part of Administration); each opens as an empty chat window and the inbox (``ui_templates.open``), the rest is offered in "add window". A conversation knows the application it was started in (``conversations.application``) and the chat window shows that application's own conversation. The **inbox** (``inbox_items``) holds what happened and wants to be read, each item with its application: reading it files it in the corner of its application unless it is pinned (the board) or urgent and not settled; open questions of the memory, proposals waiting for a click, findings of the second look and ended background tasks become items by themselves (``inbox.sync`` looks at the places that already hold them, so no service had to learn about the inbox) and an item whose matter is settled elsewhere is resolved. The board shows the pinned items, what is at work, the shelf of reports and, for an admin, offline machines.

**Why.** Every decision about where something is seen was made in two places (two UIs, two chat windows); a feature reached one and not the other. One surface and one inbox end that, and applications are the unit later steps (own memory, own rights, hand-over) hang on. Deriving items from their sources keeps the inbox right when an event is missed.
