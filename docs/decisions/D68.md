# D68 — Marks and the regular re-check

> **Status (2026-11-25):** Replaced by [D111](D111.md): marks, the board and the re-check are removed (plan step 1.16).

**Decision.** **Marks and the regular re-check (section 3.7).** An inbox item carries marks (``item_marks``: comment, Post-It, sticker, highlight; author the person, an application or ``recheck``): data on the item, never a change of its text, scoped to the person, gone with the item. The re-check (``recheck.py``) takes every pinned answer that came back from a hand-over, sends the same dossier again as the person who pinned it (their rights, the target application's tools, read-only), compares the new summary with the old (a model, or equal text without one) and leaves a sticker "still correct" or a Post-It "outdated"; only a change raises a new inbox item. Once per ``recheck_interval_hours``, at most ``recheck_max`` per round, one at a time per item; the last three of its own marks per item are kept.

**Why.** A pinned answer is a claim about a system that changes; checking it again must neither rewrite the claim nor flood the person.
