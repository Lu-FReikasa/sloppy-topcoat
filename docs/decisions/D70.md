# D70 — The desktop replaces the shells and the looks

**Decision.** **The desktop replaces the shells and the looks (docs/DESKTOP.md).** One look (one hue the person picks), no menu bar: a desktop with a taskbar at the bottom, programs that open, minimise, maximise and tile themselves, a command palette (Ctrl+K) and keys (Ctrl+Enter, Alt+1..9, Escape). A program is a built-in workspace template: Inbox, Chat, Research, Development, Incognito, Administration, Settings; its modules are its views in the order of its navigation. What is open lives in `users.ui_prefs.desktop`, clamped by the hub. Removed: the five looks (`users.look`, `/api/me/look`), the theme controls, the Terminal and Schreibtisch shells and their per-shell layout documents (`ui_layouts`, `ui_templates.layouts`, `/api/ui/layouts`, the preset routes, `proto/ui/layout.schema.json`), the layout-document window. Inbox, Incognito and Settings are every person's, whatever their profile. Migration 0064.

**Why.** Two designs side by side, a menu bar with everything and a list of 25 windows made the interface feel like several products. A desktop with a few programs is one picture, and the settings of a program sit in that program.
