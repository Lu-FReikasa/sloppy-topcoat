# D71 — Nachlese and emoji answers are policy nodes too

> **Status (2026-11-25):** Replaced like [D67](D67.md): the keys are part of `chat_policy`.

**Decision.** **Nachlese and emoji answers are policy nodes too; the System page points at the chat policy.** `policy_review` and `policy_reactions` say, per effort, whether a finished answer is read again in the background and whether an acknowledgement may get an emoji; a flow without them leaves the hub's settings (`review_enabled`, `reactions_enabled`) in charge. The other parameters stay technical settings of Administration › System (gateway timeouts, intervals, limits of background tasks): they do not decide how an answer is made.

**Why.** Only what shapes an answer belongs in the flow; moving the technical knobs there would hide them, not simplify them.
