# D90 — Speed decides on its own, quality only suggests

> **Status (2026-11-25):** Shelved (the maintainer, 2026-11-25). Not built. "Quality never changes the model by itself" holds; with house models ([D117](D117.md)) there is little to choose. Comparable numbers come from the bench and the measured runs of plan step 1.19.

**Decision.** **Speed decides on its own, quality only suggests (not built, SCHEDULING section 7).** The hub keeps measurements per application, model and node. Measured speed may change which node runs a job. It never changes the model. Comparable quality comes from a bench with deterministic scoring, and the GUI offers its result as a suggestion the person takes with one click.

**Why.** Everyday quality signals cannot be compared: models get different questions, reviewers change, and feedback is sparse (6 messages on the production hub, 2026-10-30). Letting them steer would hide the choice from the person and could lock in a measurement error, as the inflated speeds of real answers showed.
