# D91 — The decision log is for administrators

**Decision.** **The record of what the hub decided (SCH-03 step 3, OBS-01) is visible to administrators only (the record is built, D95; the admin view is OBS-01 and not built).** An administrator sees every job with its caller, the model and node asked for and chosen, the source of the pick, the waiting time and the outcome. Content never goes into the record. A person sees, as today, what concerns their own chat: which model answered and which checked, and that a job waits. Someone else's chat shows as "a chat of someone else" with no title.

**Why.** The record answers "why did this run there", which is a question about the machines and the rules, not about a person's conversation. Admins need all of it to tune the table; everyone else gains nothing from it and could infer what others do. Decided by the maintainer on 2026-10-31 (it was the proposal in the handover of 2026-10-30).
